# Engineering Principles

- **Economic utility, not profit at any cost.** Optimize for risk-adjusted value
  with uncertainty, costs and explainability; do not claim unproven profitability.
- **Scanners discover; TI advises; TM governs; the broker is truth.** TI never
  places, modifies or cancels broker orders.
- **Baseline before enrichment.** Preserve deterministic A2 evidence, NO_TRADE
  and fingerprints as a visible control, not a layer that Agents replace.
- **Evidence before inference.** Hypotheses may motivate governed research, but
  only sourced, normalized, provenance/authority/PIT-admitted facts become evidence.
- **No authority through presence.** Installed, registered, enabled, authorized
  and successful are different states; a capability or UI cannot grant rights.
- **No silent scope shrinkage.** Trusted versioned policy owns required scope,
  not registry membership. Required absence remains explicit in completeness.
- **Absence is not negative evidence.** Optional enrichment may be absent without
  destroying the baseline; failure is not a market opinion.
- **HOT ⇒ COLD ⇒ STRUCTURAL.** STRUCTURAL is the base guarantee; COLD and HOT
  require progressively stronger guarantees. Reverse implications do not follow.
  Required/optional scope is independent of replacement lifecycle.
- **Shared evidence != shared analysis state.** Share only compatible authorized,
  entitled, fresh, semantically and point-in-time scoped evidence.
- **Watchlist is organization; mandate is semantics.** Subscribers own intent
  and optional groups; deduplicate evidence acquisition, not subscriber intent.
- **Monitoring is governed orchestration, not trade execution.** A5 intent is not
  a job; the authoritative future runtime design is not an implemented daemon.
- **Non-action is valid.** WAIT, NO_TRADE, AVOID, ABSTAIN, CONFLICTED and
  INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE need honest reasons, not tuning to force activity.
- **Position advice is prospective, not operational truth.** Original entry
  rationale is optional; A5 still needs admitted A4 support for actionable advice.
  Posture, thesis health, recommendation and supplied lifecycle remain distinct.
- **Horizon and time are first-class.** Preserve horizon, evidence time, cutoff,
  freshness and lifetime separately. Reject naive datetimes; normalize to
  Asia/Kolkata, with ordinary +05:30 ISO-8601 JSON.
- **Replay is not policy comparison.** Historical replay must not acquire later
  capabilities/evidence. Preserve old hashes; compare policies in separate runs.
- **Bound reasoning and cost.** Use deterministic computation where possible;
  model-backed judgment requires explicit acceptance. Unknown cost is not zero.
