# Milestones

## TIAF_TGT0 — project bootstrap

Establish packaging, documentation, configuration, namespace boundaries, and a
local test baseline. No trading or agent capability is included.

## TIAF_A0 — domain contracts and foundation

Define stable, versioned domain contracts and the shared foundation needed by
later capabilities.

## TIAF_A1 — data foundation

Introduce validated market-data inputs, normalization, timestamps, provenance,
and data-quality behavior.

## TIAF_A2 — deterministic baseline

Build testable deterministic computations and baseline decisions without agent
or LLM dependence.

## TIAF_A3 — planner and specialist-agent MVP

Add bounded planning and specialist interpretation behind explicit contracts.

## TIAF_A4 — arbitration and adversarial review

Reconcile competing evidence and introduce skeptical review of proposed
conclusions.

## TIAF_A5 — position-intelligence MVP

Provide forward-looking analysis for existing and adopted positions.

## TIAF_A6 — option-expression intelligence

Select and evaluate option expressions independently of underlying selection.

## TIAF_A7 — evaluation and benchmarking

Measure quality, calibration, stability, latency, and outcome attribution with
reproducible benchmarks and replay.

## TIAF_A8 — TradeMonitor integration

Deliver structured intelligence to TradeMonitor while preserving its exclusive
risk and execution authority.

## TIAF_A9 — scanner integration

Connect discovery sensors without coupling them to interpretation or authority.

## TIAF_A10 — production hardening

Harden security, observability, reliability, deployment, recovery, and
operational governance.
